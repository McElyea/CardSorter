# Licensing Status

CardSorter is intended to be open source, but the project has not yet selected or
added its final software, hardware, documentation, and CAD licenses. Until explicit
license files are added, do not assume that public availability alone grants reuse
rights.

The repositories under `references/hobbyist-projects` are third-party research
snapshots. Their original licenses, or absence of a license, continue to govern
their contents. They must not be copied into CardSorter implementation folders
without a deliberate compatibility and attribution review. See
[hobbyist/SOURCES.md](hobbyist/SOURCES.md) for the recorded status.
